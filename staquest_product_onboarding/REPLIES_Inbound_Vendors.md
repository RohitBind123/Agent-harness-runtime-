# Inbound replies — send this week

Three people are waiting. One of them asked for a price a month ago. These drafts assume
**no product exists yet** and that you deliver the first ones by hand.

**The offer you are selling (manual, deliverable in an afternoon with Claude):**

> **Verified Profile + AI Visibility Report — $500 one-time, or $299/mo**
> 1. Verified, vendor-corrected StaQuest profile with a freshness date
> 2. Appearance in the relevant `/alternatives` and `/compare` pages, on merit
> 3. A written report: which AI engines mention you today for the 20 buying queries in
>    your category, who gets cited instead, and the specific reasons why
> 4. A prioritised fix list
>
> **Not for sale, and say so in every conversation:** ranking position, dimension scores,
> competitor removal.

Charge from message one. A price is the fastest qualifier you have, and free listings
attract exactly the vendors least worth having.

## The 15 discovery calls

Pick 15 tools already in your catalogue with visible marketing budgets (recently funded,
running ads, hiring growth roles). Cold email:

> Subject: You're on StaQuest — quick question about your comparison data
>
> Hi [name] — [Tool] is on StaQuest, where buyers compare it against [competitor 1] and
> [competitor 2]. We're about to open vendor accounts and I'd rather design them around
> what you'd actually use than guess.
>
> 15 minutes? Not a pitch — I'll show you what buyers do on your page either way.

**Ask these five, in this order, and shut up after each:**
1. How do you find out today that a buyer shortlisted you against a competitor?
2. What would you pay to know who you're compared against and **why you lose**?
3. Do you know whether ChatGPT recommends you? How would you find out?
4. What do you currently pay G2, Capterra or an agency — and what do you get for it?
5. If I could fix one thing about how [Tool] shows up publicly, what should it be?

**Log every answer verbatim.** Question 2 sets your Layer 2 price. Question 4 sets your
ceiling. Question 5 writes the roadmap.

## Sending discipline

- Send all three replies **the same day**. They've waited long enough.
- Don't offer a discount before anyone objects to the price.
- If someone says "too expensive," ask what they pay G2 — the number is usually 10x.
- Every conversation, without exception, includes the sentence: **"We don't sell ranking."**
  It's the differentiator, and repetition is how it becomes the brand.
